Privacy

Your Supabase URL and anon key are stored locally in your browser and are only sent to your own Supabase project's Edge Function for analysis. The Sarvam AI API key stays server-side inside the Edge Function and never touches your browser. Your text is never stored, logged, or shared with any third party.
