How to use

  1. Click the LinguaAI icon in your browser toolbar to open the mini editor.
  2. Or focus any text field on any webpage — a small green FAB appears. Click it to analyze.
  3. Right-click selected text → "Check grammar with LinguaAI".
  4. Keyboard shortcut: Ctrl+Shift+L (Win/Linux) or Cmd+Shift+L (Mac) to check the active field.
  5. Open the popup in a full tab: Ctrl+Shift+Y / Cmd+Shift+Y.
